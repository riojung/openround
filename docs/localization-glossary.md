# OpenRound localization glossary

This glossary defines product meaning and translator context. It does not prescribe one universal
translation: reviewers may choose the clearest established term in each market, but the same term
must remain consistent across navigation, authoring, delivery, reports, help, and support copy.

| Source term           | Product meaning and translation guidance                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Round                 | An OpenRound question-led live or self-paced activity. Treat it as a named product artifact, not merely one turn of a game.                                                                              |
| Presentation          | A live instructional deck containing content slides and interactive question blocks. Do not translate it as a static exported file.                                                                      |
| Recovery Loop         | The complete OpenRound pattern: diagnose, intervene, and recheck. Treat as a named product concept and preserve the sense of learning recovery.                                                          |
| diagnostic question   | A question used to identify current understanding or a misconception before an intervention. It is not a medical diagnosis.                                                                              |
| intervention          | Facilitator-provided explanation, cue, example, or activity between a diagnostic and its recheck. Avoid punitive meanings.                                                                               |
| recheck               | A linked follow-up question used to measure change after an intervention. Keep it distinct from retry, retake, and review.                                                                               |
| evidence              | Responses and diagnostic-to-recheck change that inform a facilitation decision. It is not proof of attendance or content exposure.                                                                       |
| facilitator           | The person authoring, hosting, or guiding an activity. Prefer an inclusive professional term rather than assuming teacher or manager.                                                                    |
| participant           | A person joining a live session or assignment, usually without a workspace account. Do not imply workspace membership.                                                                                   |
| workspace             | The facilitator team's private operational space for artifacts, delivery, results, settings, and collaboration.                                                                                          |
| assignment            | A self-paced delivery of a published Round. Presentation assignments are not supported in the current implementation.                                                                                    |
| starter               | A first-party, editable starting artifact. Keep it distinct from a locked example or paid marketplace item.                                                                                              |
| trusted source        | Material supplied or approved by the user and used to ground proposed content. Do not imply that OpenRound independently verified its truth.                                                             |
| Question Health       | Deterministic, advisory checks on authored questions. Preserve its distinction from a participant assessment or an automatic quality verdict.                                                            |
| finding               | A specific Question Health observation with evidence and a suggested action. It need not be a validation error or publication blocker.                                                                   |
| dismiss / reopen      | Save or reverse a reasoned decision about a matching advisory finding. Distinguish this from dismissing an audience Q&A item or deleting content.                                                        |
| draft revision        | One saved state of an editable artifact. Apply and undo refer to this saved revision, not a published version or a live session.                                                                         |
| post-use observations | Descriptive aggregates from retained session reports for one exact published version, separated by compatible settings. Do not imply causal evidence or learner profiling.                               |
| Decision replay       | A recorded timeline of facilitator actions and shown insight measurements in a Round report. It is not a video replay or reconstructed participant behavior.                                             |
| Flex                  | Whole-room live timing without response countdowns, deadlines, or speed bonuses; the host closes responses. Keep it distinct from a self-paced time-flex assignment or an individual accommodation pass. |
| text element          | A title or text box on a structured Presentation slide. The maximum of eight includes its one title; it is not eight boxes plus a title.                                                                 |
| position on slide     | A quick arrangement in one of nine slide regions. It complements bounded percentage-based positioning and per-region reading order.                                                                      |
| layout guides         | Editor-only grid, safe margins, and alignment cues. They are not content delivered to participants.                                                                                                      |
| Image area            | Space reserved for an attached slide image; text moves and resizes remain clear of it. Do not imply an additional editable text box.                                                                     |
| archive / restore     | Reversible Library visibility and its reversal. Neither action permanently deletes the artifact or its retained use.                                                                                     |
| delete permanently    | An owner-confirmed, irreversible removal of archived content or eligible session history. Keep the scope and retained-use blockers explicit.                                                             |
| expired session       | A session whose live access has expired, even if its history remains retained. It cannot resume; expiry does not mean the history was already deleted.                                                   |

## Style and safety notes

- Preserve stable product names, IDs, error codes, data values, citations, and user-authored content.
- Translate interface labels by meaning, not word order. Keep actions concise and use the same voice
  within a locale.
- Do not introduce promises of learning, compliance, perfect slide reproduction, or AI accuracy.
- Keep diagnostic and recheck terminology visibly distinct wherever the Recovery Loop appears.
- Preserve the scope of draft, published version, live session, and retained history in action
  labels. Do not translate archive, expiry, and permanent deletion as interchangeable outcomes.
- Use native punctuation, spacing, capitalization, number, date, and list conventions.
- When a concept is ambiguous, add a translator question instead of silently choosing a new product
  meaning.
