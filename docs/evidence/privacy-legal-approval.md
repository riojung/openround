# Launch-market privacy and legal approval record

This template records a decision, not legal advice. Do not commit privileged communications,
personal contact details, signatures, contract terms, customer names, regulator correspondence, or
private document URLs. Keep the written opinion and source documents in the approved restricted
legal system and reference only a redacted identifier or checksum here.
